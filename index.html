Your first appointment

No guesswork. Just a plan built for your skin.

A facial is amazing to fit into your monthly routine — but without real knowledge of your skin, the benefits don't last. Every journey starts with understanding what's happening beneath the surface.

    Your lifestyle, history and goals — so treatment fits you, not a trend.

  2. Professional skin analysis

    Detailed imaging of your skin at different layers: sensitivity, spots, texture, pores, UV damage, microbiome and more.

  3. Your digital skin report

    A personalised report after your visit — with free updates throughout your plan.

    A tailored treatment on the day, daily personalised homecare, and a contact number for continued support.
